# Harborview weighs a downtown congestion charge

*A fictional scenario for trying out SwarmSight.*

The Harborview City Council will vote next month on a plan to charge drivers $12 a day to enter the downtown core during weekday rush hours. Mayor Elena Ruiz backs the plan and says the money would fund forty new electric buses and safer bike lanes. Councillor Marcus Webb opposes the charge and warns it would hurt working families who commute from the outer districts.

The Harborview Transit Authority published a study estimating that traffic in the core would fall by 18 percent and bus journeys would speed up by a quarter. Dana Okafor, who leads the Transit Authority, praised the plan as the biggest boost to public transport in a generation.

The Downtown Business Association opposes the charge. Its president, Priya Shah, says shop owners fear losing customers to the suburban malls and warned that some stores could close. The Harborview Chamber of Commerce is split, with larger employers in favour and small retailers against.

Clean Air Harborview, a coalition of parents and doctors, welcomes the plan and has organised rallies outside City Hall. Its spokesperson, Tom Brennan, points to asthma rates near the harbour freeway that are double the city average.

The Rideshare Drivers Union says its members would face higher costs and has threatened a protest convoy. Union organiser Luis Moreno criticized the plan as a tax on the people who keep the city moving.

Local newspaper the Harborview Courier ran an editorial cautiously in favour, arguing the plan should include discounts for low-income residents. A poll by Northshore University found 46 percent of residents support the charge, 41 percent oppose it, and the rest are undecided. Professor Hannah Lee, who ran the poll, said support has grown since a similar scheme succeeded in a neighbouring city.

Outer District Residents have formed a group called Fair Fares Now to push for exemptions. Its founder, Ray Castillo, says he is not against cleaner air but will oppose any plan that does not protect commuters without good bus service.
